About

I am a Technical Lead at PayPal with more than 16 years of experience designing and delivering large-scale distributed systems. I currently lead the cloud transformation of the Enterprise Risk and Compliance domain, having re-architected more than 15 applications for the cloud and reduced infrastructure costs by roughly 40%. The program spans scalability, performance and data-migration strategy. I also represent the broader regulatory compliance domain and lead the design of new platforms built to meet regulatory requirements from day one. My earlier experience includes engineering roles at Amazon, Workday, eBay and other technology companies.

My work centers on distributed web applications and services, and increasingly on AI/ML solutions. I provide architectural direction and technical leadership that carry complex initiatives from planning through delivery. I partner closely with product, design and quality engineering teams to shape and ship features, and I work across organizational boundaries to keep stakeholders and partners aligned. I am known for rigorous problem-solving and for resolving difficult production issues.
